# Eval corpus attribution

The six papers the eval corpus (`corpus_manifest.json`) pins are each licensed
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
as shown on each paper's arXiv abstract page. The PDFs are not committed; `scripts/fetch_eval_corpus.py`
downloads them. Quotations from them appear in this repository (golden-set answers, chunk excerpts,
recorded replay cassettes) under that licence. No endorsement by the authors is implied. Changes
made: the material is excerpted and paraphrased, and passed through a PDF parser that alters layout.
